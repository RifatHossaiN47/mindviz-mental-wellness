4-7-8 BREATHING TECHNIQUE

OVERVIEW:
The 4-7-8 breathing technique is a simple yet powerful relaxation method developed by Dr. Andrew Weil. It helps reduce anxiety and promote calmness by regulating your breath.

HOW IT WORKS:
This technique activates your parasympathetic nervous system, which helps your body relax and reduces stress hormones.

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Sit comfortably with your back straight
2. Place the tip of your tongue against the ridge behind your upper front teeth
3. Exhale completely through your mouth, making a whoosh sound
4. Close your mouth and inhale quietly through your nose for 4 counts
5. Hold your breath for 7 counts
6. Exhale completely through your mouth for 8 counts, making a whoosh sound
7. This completes one breath cycle
8. Repeat for 4 cycles initially, gradually increasing to 8 cycles

BENEFITS:
- Reduces anxiety and stress
- Helps with sleep
- Manages anger responses
- Lowers blood pressure
- Improves focus

BEST FOR:
- Anxiety attacks
- Before sleep
- During stressful moments
- Exam preparation

DURATION: 5-10 minutes
DIFFICULTY: Easy
EVIDENCE: Widely recommended by mental health professionals worldwide
