PROGRESSIVE MUSCLE RELAXATION (PMR)

OVERVIEW:
Progressive Muscle Relaxation is a technique that involves tensing and then relaxing different muscle groups in your body. Developed by Dr. Edmund Jacobson in the 1920s, it helps release physical tension and reduce anxiety.

HOW IT WORKS:
By deliberately tensing and then releasing muscle groups, you become more aware of physical tension and learn to release it.

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Find a quiet, comfortable place to sit or lie down
2. Take a few deep breaths to center yourself
3. Starting with your feet, tense the muscles for 5 seconds
4. Release the tension suddenly and notice the difference
5. Rest for 10 seconds before moving to the next muscle group
6. Work your way up through your body:
   - Feet and calves
   - Thighs
   - Buttocks and hips
   - Stomach
   - Chest
   - Hands and forearms
   - Upper arms
   - Shoulders
   - Neck
   - Face

TIPS:
- Don't tense too hard - moderate tension is enough
- Focus on the feeling of release
- Breathe normally throughout
- Practice regularly for best results

BENEFITS:
- Reduces muscle tension
- Decreases anxiety
- Improves sleep quality
- Helps with chronic pain
- Lowers stress hormones

BEST FOR:
- Physical tension
- Sleep problems
- Chronic stress
- Anxiety disorders

DURATION: 10-15 minutes
DIFFICULTY: Easy
EVIDENCE: Supported by WHO and NHS guidelines
