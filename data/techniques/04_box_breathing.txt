BOX BREATHING (4-4-4-4 TECHNIQUE)

OVERVIEW:
Box breathing, also called square breathing, is used by Navy SEALs to stay calm and focused in high-stress situations. It's simple but incredibly effective.

HOW IT WORKS:
This technique brings your attention to the present moment and away from stressful thoughts while regulating your nervous system.

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Sit upright in a comfortable position
2. Visualize a box or square
3. Follow these four equal steps:
   - INHALE for 4 counts (going up one side of the box)
   - HOLD for 4 counts (across the top)
   - EXHALE for 4 counts (down the other side)
   - HOLD for 4 counts (across the bottom)
4. Repeat for 5-10 minutes

VISUALIZATION:
Imagine tracing a square:
   HOLD (4) ────────── INHALE (4)
      │                    │
      │                    │
   EXHALE (4) ────────── HOLD (4)

TIPS:
- Keep counts steady and consistent
- Don't strain - breathe naturally within the count
- Focus on the visualization to enhance effects
- Practice daily for best results

BENEFITS:
- Immediate stress relief
- Improved focus and concentration
- Better emotional regulation
- Reduced physical symptoms of anxiety
- Enhanced performance under pressure

BEST FOR:
- Pre-exam anxiety
- High-pressure situations
- Panic attacks
- Performance anxiety

DURATION: 5-10 minutes
DIFFICULTY: Easy
EVIDENCE: Used by military and first responders worldwide
