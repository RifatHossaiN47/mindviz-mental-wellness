5-4-3-2-1 GROUNDING TECHNIQUE

OVERVIEW:
The 5-4-3-2-1 technique is a mindfulness exercise that helps ground you in the present moment using your five senses. It's particularly effective for anxiety and panic attacks.

HOW IT WORKS:
By engaging all your senses, you interrupt anxious thoughts and bring your awareness to the present moment, away from worries about the future or past.

INSTRUCTIONS:

STEP 1 - ACKNOWLEDGE 5 THINGS YOU CAN SEE
Look around and name 5 things you can see:
- "I see my laptop"
- "I see a blue pen"
- "I see the window"
- "I see a book"
- "I see my phone"

STEP 2 - ACKNOWLEDGE 4 THINGS YOU CAN TOUCH
Focus on 4 things you can physically feel:
- "I feel the chair supporting me"
- "I feel my feet on the floor"
- "I feel the texture of my clothes"
- "I feel the temperature of the air"

STEP 3 - ACKNOWLEDGE 3 THINGS YOU CAN HEAR
Listen carefully and identify 3 sounds:
- "I hear the fan running"
- "I hear birds outside"
- "I hear my own breathing"

STEP 4 - ACKNOWLEDGE 2 THINGS YOU CAN SMELL
Notice 2 scents around you:
- "I smell coffee"
- "I smell fresh air"
(If you can't smell anything, name 2 favorite scents)

STEP 5 - ACKNOWLEDGE 1 THING YOU CAN TASTE
Focus on 1 taste:
- "I taste mint from my toothpaste"
- "I taste my last meal"
(Or imagine a favorite taste)

TIPS:
- Speak out loud if possible
- Take your time with each sense
- Be specific and detailed
- Repeat if needed

BENEFITS:
- Immediate anxiety relief
- Stops panic attacks
- Increases present-moment awareness
- Reduces dissociation
- No preparation needed

BEST FOR:
- Panic attacks
- Acute anxiety
- Overwhelming emotions
- Dissociation
- Emergency situations

DURATION: 3-5 minutes
DIFFICULTY: Very Easy
EVIDENCE: Widely recommended by therapists and crisis counselors
