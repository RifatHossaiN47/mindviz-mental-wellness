BODY SCAN MEDITATION

OVERVIEW:
Body scan meditation is a mindfulness practice that involves systematically focusing on different parts of your body. It's excellent for releasing tension and connecting mind and body.

HOW IT WORKS:
By bringing conscious attention to each part of your body, you become aware of tension and can actively release it while promoting overall relaxation.

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Lie down or sit comfortably
2. Close your eyes and take 3 deep breaths
3. Bring your attention to your feet
4. Notice any sensations - warmth, coolness, tingling, tension
5. Breathe into that area and imagine tension releasing
6. Slowly move your attention up through your body:
   - Feet and toes
   - Ankles and calves
   - Knees and thighs
   - Hips and buttocks
   - Lower back and abdomen
   - Upper back and chest
   - Shoulders
   - Arms and hands
   - Neck
   - Face and head
7. Spend 30-60 seconds on each area
8. Finish by breathing and noticing your whole body

GUIDED SCRIPT:
"Focus on your feet. Notice how they feel. Are they warm or cool? Tense or relaxed? Don't judge, just observe. Breathe deeply and imagine sending your breath to your feet. On the exhale, release any tension..."

TIPS:
- Don't rush
- You might fall asleep - that's okay
- If your mind wanders, gently return to the body part
- Practice regularly for best results
- Can be done anytime, anywhere

BENEFITS:
- Deep relaxation
- Better body awareness
- Improved sleep
- Pain management
- Stress reduction
- Enhanced mindfulness

BEST FOR:
- Insomnia
- Chronic pain
- General stress
- Mindfulness practice
- Body tension

DURATION: 10-20 minutes
DIFFICULTY: Easy
EVIDENCE: Core practice in Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR), validated by extensive research
